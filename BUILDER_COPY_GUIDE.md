## Validation Wording

Critical:

- `Add at least one deterministic condition.`
- `Choose at least one alert destination.`
- `Fix the critical details before activation.`
- `Validation is required before monitoring starts.`

Warning:

- `One condition may be noisy. Consider a filter or cooldown.`
- `Many required conditions may produce very few matches.`
- `This monitor uses a single timeframe.`

Ready:

- `Validation passed.`
- `Ready to start.`
- `No local conflicts detected.`

## Non-Advisory Wording

- `No monitoring starts until confirmed.`
- `Context only. TraceEdge does not place trades.`
- `Suggestion only. No strategy fields were changed.`
- `The monitor can still be valid; the market may not meet it now.`

Do not describe monitor output as guaranteed financial advice or future prediction.
