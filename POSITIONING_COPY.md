# Positioning Copy

## Do Not Position As

Do not use these as primary positioning:

- AI alert builder
- crypto signal bot
- guaranteed setup finder
- profit predictor
- automated trading bot
- buy/sell recommendation service

## Primary Positioning

TraceEdge turns your trading edge into a monitored, validated, and explained strategy map.

## Sharp One-Liners

- Know what your strategy is watching, why it alerts, and why it stays silent.
- Your edge, monitored, tested, and explained.
- Describe the setup. Review the mechanics. Monitor the market.
- From prompt to proof, without handing over trading keys.
- A monitoring cockpit for the setups you already trade.

## Alert Copy

Confirmed setup:

> Setup confirmed because every required condition passed on closed market data.

Forming setup:

> Setup is forming. These conditions passed; these still need confirmation.

Invalidated setup:

> Setup invalidated because a required condition failed or price moved beyond the
> configured invalidation level.

Why no alert:

> TraceEdge reconstructed the strategy at that time. Here is what passed, what failed,
> and whether delivery, cooldown, data, or subscription rules blocked the alert.

## Safety Copy

Short:

> TraceEdge is monitoring and decision support. It does not place trades for you.

Long:

> TraceEdge converts approved strategy rules into deterministic market monitors. Alerts
> may be delayed, incomplete, or incorrect. You are responsible for verifying each setup
> and making your own trading decisions.

## Landing Page Section Copy

Prompt coverage:

> See exactly which parts of your prompt became rules, assumptions, unsupported items,
> or questions.

Proof receipts:

> Every alert includes required values, actual values, pass/fail status, exchange,
> timeframe, candle timestamp, and strategy version.

Lifecycle:

> Follow each setup from detected to forming, confirmed, entry active, invalidated,
> expired, or completed.

Diagnostics:

> Ask why an alert did not fire. TraceEdge reconstructs the conditions instead of guessing.
