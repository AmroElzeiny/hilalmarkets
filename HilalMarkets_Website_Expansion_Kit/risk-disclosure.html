Purpose of HilalMarkets

HilalMarkets is a research, screening, and market-monitoring service. It helps users explore assets screened under disclosed methodologies, create Watch Plans, observe opportunity states, receive alerts, and review evidence. It does not execute transactions or manage customer assets.

No religious, financial, legal, tax, or investment advice

Content, statuses, Watch Plan results, alerts, charts, evidence summaries, and methodology comparisons are provided for information and research. They are not a fatwa, personal Sharia advice, financial recommendation, investment advice, legal opinion, tax advice, or instruction to buy, sell, hold, stake, lend, borrow, or use any asset or service.

Digital-asset and market risk

Crypto assets can be highly volatile, illiquid, technically complex, and subject to rapid losses. Projects, networks, exchanges, custody arrangements, laws, and market conditions can change without notice. Historical behavior, readiness, a forming opportunity, or an alert does not predict future performance.

Methodology-specific Sharia screening

A status such as Eligible means the asset met the effective policy under the stated methodology, version, evidence set, and review date. It is not a universal declaration that every use of the asset is halal. Different scholars and approved methodologies may disagree. A project can also change after review.

Users should review the Evidence Passport, qualifications, methodology, source dates, and status history and seek qualified personal guidance where appropriate.

Market data, scans, alerts, and missed alerts

Data can be delayed, unavailable, incomplete, corrected, or inconsistent across providers. Network failures, exchange maintenance, rate limits, incomplete candles, symbol changes, time-zone handling, cooldowns, policy changes, and delivery incidents can prevent or delay a scan or alert. An alert is not guaranteed to arrive before, during, or after a market move.

Third-party services

HilalMarkets may depend on exchanges, data providers, Telegram, payment processors, cloud services, and other third parties. Their availability, security, terms, and data practices are outside HilalMarkets’ complete control.

Your responsibility

You remain responsible for independently evaluating an asset, methodology, opportunity, platform, transaction, custody method, and applicable law. Do not risk funds you cannot afford to lose. Never share passwords, authentication tokens, wallet seed phrases, or private keys with HilalMarkets.
