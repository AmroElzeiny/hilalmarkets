The language matters

We say “screened under a methodology,” not “universally halal.”

Islamic-finance methodologies can apply different definitions, thresholds, and evidence requirements. HilalMarkets should make those differences visible rather than replacing them with an unexplained score.

Methodology-specific

Every result names the methodology and version used.

Evidence-specific

The result depends on dated, traceable sources and completeness requirements.

Human-governed

Qualified reviewers own final methodology and status authority.

Time-specific

Projects and methodologies change; history is retained instead of overwritten.

Screening workflow

From evidence to a versioned status.

The public page explains the process once. Each asset’s Passport then carries the specific detail.

01

Define the approved methodology

Publish scope, rule definitions, evidence requirements, statuses, governance, and version history.

02

Collect and structure evidence

Review primary activity, token utility, lending and yield exposure, staking structure, treasury, governance, and disclosure completeness.

03

Apply the methodology

Rules may be computed or supported by technology, but the final religious-governance decision remains with approved reviewers.

04

Publish the Evidence Passport

Show status, qualification, review date, authority, sources, outcomes, and status history.

05

Watch for compliance drift

Material project or methodology changes can create a new review case, update the status, and affect Watch Plans.

Important limitation

A production claim requires real governance and real data.

A development methodology, placeholder assessment, or AI-generated conclusion must never be displayed as a genuine halal or Sharia status. Public launch requires approved methodology governance, real reviewed evidence, review operations, and clear legal disclosures.
