Scope

This notice explains how HilalMarkets handles information when you visit public pages, create an account, configure Watch Plans, receive alerts, contact support, or use related services.

Information we may collect

Account and contact information

Name, email address, authentication records, language, time zone, account status, and support communications.

Product configuration and activity

Selected methodology, approved statuses, watchlists, Watch Plan configuration, notification preferences, market-check records, opportunity and alert interactions, and stable evidence references.

Technical and security information

IP address, device and browser information, session and security events, logs, error references, consent choices, and fraud-prevention signals.

Billing information

Plan, invoices, payment status, and processor references. Full payment-card details should be handled by the payment provider, not stored by HilalMarkets.

How information may be used

  • Provide, secure, and improve the service.
  • Resolve screened universes and run approved Watch Plans.
  • Deliver opportunity and compliance notifications.
  • Manage subscriptions, support, incidents, and legal obligations.
  • Measure aggregate activation, reliability, and product use with appropriate consent.

Analytics and private product data

Optional website analytics should not receive passwords, credentials, wallet keys, raw private Watch Plan prompts, detailed strategy text, reviewer notes, support attachments, or direct personal identifiers. Consent choices are applied through the preference center and Google Consent Mode configuration.

Service providers and disclosures

Information may be processed by carefully selected hosting, email, alert-delivery, analytics, support, payment, and security providers under appropriate contracts. Production copy must list or link to material subprocessors and explain international transfers.

Retention

Retention should be limited to the time needed for service delivery, security, billing, legal duties, evidence integrity, and dispute handling. Production schedules must specify periods by data category and distinguish account deletion from legally required or immutable audit records.

Your choices and rights

Depending on jurisdiction, users may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, or objection, and may withdraw optional consent. The product should include data export, cookie settings, preference deletion, and account-deletion workflows.

Security

HilalMarkets should use access controls, encryption, secret isolation, logging, incident response, backup protection, and regular testing. No system can guarantee absolute security.
