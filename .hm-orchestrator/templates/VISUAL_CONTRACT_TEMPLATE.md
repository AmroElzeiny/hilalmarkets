## Screenshot acceptance

For every viewport:
- expected screenshot path;
- exact elements to compare;
- tolerance only for rendering differences, never for wrong token/layout/content;
- vision review required.

## Escalation triggers

Escalate to Claude if:
- source-of-truth designs conflict;
- exact token/size is missing;
- requested visual behavior needs a new design decision;
- screenshot cannot be produced;
- vision reviewer cannot inspect the screenshot;
- reviewer sees material mismatch after two repair attempts.
