# Product Positioning

TraceEdge is a monitoring and decision-support platform for crypto spot traders.
It does not place trades, promise profits, or predict the future.

Core message:

> See your setup forming before it confirms.

The product helps a user describe a setup once, approve the structured interpretation, and then monitor supported spot markets with deterministic rule evaluation.

Primary differentiators:

- Strategy Translator: turns natural language into explicit rules for approval.
- Near-Miss Radar: ranks markets that are close to completing the setup.
- Setup Replay: reconstructs why a move did or did not trigger an alert.
- Proof Receipts: records required values, actual values, data freshness, and rule status.
- Alert Trust Score: explains the quality of the evidence behind an alert.
- Market Coverage Score: shows whether enough markets were actually scanned.

Positioning guardrails:

- Do not call alerts guaranteed signals.
- Do not publish fake win rates or countdown pressure.
- Always say the user makes the final trading decision.
- Keep first-touch onboarding simple: landing page, Telegram, then dashboard for detail.
