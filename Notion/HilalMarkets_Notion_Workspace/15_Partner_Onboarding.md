# 🤝 Partner Onboarding and Collaboration

> **Workspace status:** Updated 17 July 2026. Product name: **HilalMarkets**.

## Partner types

### Sharia governance partner
**Needed for:** methodology approval, reviewer qualification, difficult cases, public wording, change policy, appeals.  
**HilalMarkets provides:** retained evidence, criterion workspace, audit trail, Passport publication, assignment and SLA, drift detection.  
**Boundary:** no technology output can replace the partner’s qualified decision.

### Source or research partner
**Needed for:** reliable evidence, project identity, governance, tokenomics, treasury, use-specific facts.  
**HilalMarkets provides:** source registry, snapshots, hashes, change detection, dossier tooling, factual-problem reports.  
**Boundary:** source licensing and permitted retention/display must be documented.

### Market-data or exchange partner
**Needed for:** reliable spot symbols, OHLC data, delisting status, liquidity, and potentially commercial display rights.  
**HilalMarkets provides:** policy-gated monitoring and evidence-led customer experience.  
**Boundary:** no trading credentials or execution in the initial platform.

### Distribution partner
Examples: Islamic-finance app, educator, bank, broker, community, university, or fintech ecosystem.  
**Needed for:** user access, credibility, interviews, pilots.  
**HilalMarkets provides:** co-branded education, screened-market workflow, monitored opportunities, evidence links.  
**Boundary:** no guaranteed-return or anonymous signal promotion.

### Accelerator or investor
**Needed for:** capital, market access, team, data/regulatory introductions, commercial discipline.  
**HilalMarkets provides:** working product foundation, focused category, regional relevance, governance roadmap, recurring SaaS model.

### Engineering or AI partner
**Needed for:** reliability, security, data systems, analytics, product delivery.  
**Boundary:** must follow deterministic authority, privacy, source, and no-ruling rules.

## Partner onboarding pack

Share:

1. Company Overview
2. Product System
3. Audience and Jobs
4. Governance and Evidence
5. Technology Architecture
6. Risk and Legal Readiness
7. Relevant data-room documents
8. Proposed scope and success criteria
9. Confidentiality and IP terms
10. Named decision makers and communication cadence

## Due diligence questions

### Governance partner
- What is the methodology authority and scope?
- How are digital assets and use cases treated?
- What evidence is required?
- What reviewer qualifications and conflicts policy apply?
- How often are statuses reviewed?
- How should disagreements and appeals be handled?
- Which public wording is acceptable?

### Data/source partner
- May HilalMarkets store, hash, display, summarize, and monitor the content?
- What are attribution and redistribution requirements?
- What freshness and service-level commitments exist?
- What happens after schema or URL changes?
- How are corrections communicated?

### Distribution partner
- Who is the target customer?
- Who owns the customer and support relationship?
- What claims may each party make?
- What data is shared?
- How is consent handled?
- What defines pilot success?

## Partnership success metrics

- reviewed assets published;
- evidence freshness;
- sourced users activated;
- Watchlists created;
- seven-day retention;
- support burden;
- partner-sourced revenue;
- qualified introductions;
- pilot renewal or expansion.
