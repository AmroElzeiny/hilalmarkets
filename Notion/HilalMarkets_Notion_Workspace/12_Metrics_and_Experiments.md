# 📊 Metrics and Experiments

> **Workspace status:** Updated 17 July 2026. Product name: **HilalMarkets**.

## North-star metric

**Weekly useful monitored outcomes**

Count active Watchlists that successfully evaluated an eligible screened universe and led the user to inspect at least one opportunity, alert proof, investigation, or compliance change during the week.

This avoids optimizing for empty account creation or raw alert volume.

## Activation funnel

1. Landing CTA clicked
2. Signup started
3. Email verified
4. Screened asset viewed
5. Passport opened
6. Watchlist draft created
7. Interpretation approved
8. Market check completed
9. Delivery connected and tested
10. Watchlist activated
11. First scheduled scan completed
12. First useful outcome inspected

## First experiments

| Experiment | Hypothesis | Success signal |
|---|---|---|
| Screened asset first vs Watchlist first onboarding | Passport trust may increase activation | Higher Watchlist activation and lower abandonment |
| Three guided opportunity types | Beginners need bounded choices | Faster approval and fewer clarification loops |
| Telegram test before activation | Delivery proof increases confidence | Higher first-week retention |
| Compliance Drift preview | Ongoing compliance is a retention driver | Higher return intent and paid interest |
| Plain-language card vs indicator-heavy card | Outcome language is easier | Higher evidence opens and comprehension |
| Free tier with one Watchlist | First value can be reached without payment | Meaningful activation without runaway infrastructure cost |

## Review cadence

- Daily: provider health, scans, delivery, source jobs, payment failures.
- Weekly: funnel, user interviews, churn reasons, support themes, experiments.
- Monthly: pricing, cohort retention, governance SLA, competitor changes, accelerator progress.
- Quarterly: product scope, methodology policy, data licensing, expansion readiness.
