# 📣 Go-To-Market and Growth

> **Workspace status:** Updated 17 July 2026. Product name: **HilalMarkets**.

## Market-entry thesis

Lead with one coherent job:

> Help Muslim investors find and follow screened crypto spot opportunities without watching every chart.

Do not market separate “Sharia tools” and “technical tools.” Screening defines the eligible market; guided Watchlists create the monitoring experience.

## Primary positioning

**Headline:** Know what fits your values. Know when it matters.  
**Description:** Explore screened spot assets and let HilalMarkets watch for the market changes you care about—then understand every opportunity, alert, and compliance change.

## Launch sequence

### 1. Trust foundation
- Publish How We Screen.
- Name the governing authority and methodology.
- Show sample current and historical Evidence Passports.
- Explain what is not covered.
- Demonstrate review, change, correction, and appeal processes.

### 2. Private beta
Recruit the guided active Muslim-investor persona through direct interviews, trusted adviser introductions, search intent, and controlled partner referrals.

The beta should test:
- Can users explain what they want to watch?
- Do they understand methodology-specific status?
- Can they activate without expert help?
- Do they trust the evidence?
- Do they return for opportunities or compliance changes?
- What makes them willing to pay?

### 3. Search-led acquisition
Initial high-intent themes:
- Sharia-screened crypto assets;
- halal crypto monitoring;
- evidence behind crypto screening;
- crypto compliance status changes;
- screened crypto alerts;
- spot-only Muslim investor tools.

Avoid claims such as:
- guaranteed halal;
- profitable signals;
- best trades;
- AI predicts crypto;
- never miss a winner;
- risk-free.

### 4. Product demonstrations
Use short silent demos:
Screened Market → Passport Quick View → guided Watchlist → Check the Market Now → forming opportunity → Telegram proof → Compliance Change.

### 5. Partnerships
Prioritize:
- qualified Sharia advisers and institutions;
- Islamic-finance educators who value evidence;
- MENA fintech and digital-asset ecosystems;
- approved market-data and exchange partners;
- universities and research groups;
- brokers or apps seeking a screening/monitoring layer.

## Initial paid experiment

Do not split a very small budget across many platforms. After analytics and activation tracking work:

- one Google Search campaign;
- exact/phrase high-intent terms;
- one dedicated landing page;
- one conversion: **Watchlist activated**, not account created;
- strong negative keywords for signals, futures, leverage, bots, pump groups, and courses.

## Content system

### Trust content
- how screening works;
- why statuses change;
- what the Passport proves and does not prove;
- methodology disagreement;
- spot token versus staking/lending/yield use.

### Product content
- how to create a Watchlist without indicators;
- difference between one-time check and continuous monitoring;
- forming opportunity versus alert;
- why an alert did not happen;
- what happens after a status change.

### Founder and ecosystem content
- building explainable market infrastructure;
- lessons from reviewing source evidence;
- operational transparency;
- MENA and Islamic fintech development.

## Funnel

Visitor → screened asset viewed → Passport opened → account verified → Watchlist created → first scan → test alert → first opportunity/evidence view → seven-day return → paid plan.
