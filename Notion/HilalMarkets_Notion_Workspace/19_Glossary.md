# 📚 Glossary

> **Workspace status:** Updated 17 July 2026. Product name: **HilalMarkets**.

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Sharia-Screened Market | Assets allowed by the user’s effective methodology and status policy |
| Methodology | A versioned set of scope, criteria, evidence requirements, and governance |
| Assessment | A reviewed methodology-specific conclusion for an asset |
| Publication | The explicit action that makes an approved assessment customer-visible |
| Evidence Passport | Current or historical record of identity, status, reasons, sources, review, uses, and history |
| Passport Quick View | Compact modal/sheet used across market, saved assets, opportunities, changes, and proof |
| Eligible | Meets the disclosed methodology based on the reviewed evidence/version |
| Eligible with qualifications | Meets it, but important limits or qualifications apply |
| Under review | Material question or change requires review; new opportunities may be paused |
| Disputed | Approved methodologies or reviewers differ |
| Excluded | Does not meet the selected methodology |
| Insufficient information | Required evidence is not enough to conclude |
| Saved Asset | A user’s asset-level follow list, still subject to current policy |
| Watchlist | Approved measurable market conditions, screened universe, alert timing, and compliance-change behavior |
| Check the Market Now | One-time use of the same validated rules against the eligible market |
| Opportunity | One asset/timeframe instance matching part or all of a Watchlist |
| Readiness | Deterministic degree of completion; not a probability of profit |
| Opportunity Journey | Persistent sequence from detection through forming, alert, pause, or ending |
| Alert proof | Frozen market, condition, data, methodology, Passport, and delivery evidence |
| Why didn’t this alert happen? | Deterministic reconstruction of market, policy, data, cooldown, and delivery reasons |
| Compliance Change | Material source, project, identity, methodology, or evidence change |
| Compliance Drift Alert | Notification showing status change and effect on Saved Assets or Watchlists |
| Safety hold | Operational under-review overlay pending qualified review |
| Canonical asset | Verified identity independent of ticker ambiguity |
| Exchange mapping | Exact provider/exchange market linked to the canonical asset |
| Universe resolver | Single service deciding which assets may be evaluated |
| System Brain | Internal admin workspace, absent from customer navigation and public indexing |
| Deterministic engine | Code-based market evaluation with no LLM decision authority |
| Bounded Agent Control | Optional typed AI orchestration with strict tools, budgets, and prohibited actions |
| Fail closed | Missing or unsafe information blocks inclusion rather than allowing fallback |
