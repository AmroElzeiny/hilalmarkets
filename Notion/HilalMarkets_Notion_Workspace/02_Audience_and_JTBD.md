# 🎯 Audience and Jobs To Be Done

> **Workspace status:** Updated 17 July 2026. Product name: **HilalMarkets**.

## Primary audience: the guided active Muslim investor

This is the audience that harmonizes screening and market monitoring.

### Profile

- Uses or is considering crypto spot markets.
- Avoids leverage, futures, interest-based lending, and clearly prohibited project activity.
- Understands basic market language such as price level, breakout, trend, pullback, and volume.
- Does not necessarily know formulas, nested logic, or indicator implementation details.
- Wants recommendations on *how to structure monitoring*, not instructions on *what to buy*.
- Values evidence, control, and clarity more than a green badge or anonymous signal.
- Wants alerts delivered without sharing exchange trading credentials.
- May be Arabic-speaking or a non-native English speaker.

### Core job to be done

> “Help me find and follow market situations among assets that fit my selected Sharia approach, without making me become a technical expert or watch every chart.”

## Jobs, pains, and outcomes

| Job | Current pain | Desired outcome |
|---|---|---|
| Decide what to consider | Different lists and unclear reasoning | A dated Passport tied to a disclosed methodology |
| Find market activity | Too many charts and noisy lists | A small set of understandable opportunities |
| Define what matters | Idea exists only in the user’s head | Plain language translated into measurable mechanics |
| Keep watching | Manual chart checking causes missed moments | A continuous Watchlist across policy-allowed assets |
| Trust an alert | Traditional alerts show only a trigger | Exact condition, price, time, data, and Passport evidence |
| Understand a non-alert | User assumes the system failed | Clear market, policy, data, cooldown, or delivery reason |
| Stay compliant | Asset/project conditions can change | Drift alert, impact summary, and safe pause behavior |

## Secondary audience

Experienced self-directed traders can open **Advanced Controls** to inspect or edit exact indicators, timeframes, thresholds, groups, and universe settings. The advanced engine is a capability layer, not a separate product or the main landing-page promise.

## Anti-personas

HilalMarkets should not optimize the first release for:

- high-frequency scalpers requiring tick-level licensed data;
- users seeking automatic execution, leverage, futures, or copy trading;
- passive users who only want a static “halal list” and no market monitoring;
- professional institutions requiring a complete compliance API on day one;
- users demanding a universal religious ruling with no methodology context;
- communities seeking public signals with entry, stop, and target performance claims.

## Purchase and retention triggers

### Purchase triggers
- The user has more Watchlists than the free tier allows.
- The user wants continuous monitoring across a larger screened universe.
- The user needs deeper evidence history or more investigations.
- The user wants Discord, advanced mechanics, or more frequent scans.
- A compliance-change notification prevents the user from continuing with stale assumptions.

### Retention triggers
- Active opportunities are understandable and useful.
- Alerts arrive reliably with evidence.
- The user can see what is getting closer before confirmation.
- Compliance Changes create recurring value beyond one-time screening.
- The platform saves time without taking away user control.

## Language rules

Use ordinary words first:

- **Watchlist**, not “strategy engine.”
- **Opportunity**, not “candidate.”
- **Forming**, not “near-miss state.”
- **What is still missing**, not “blocker vector.”
- **Check the Market Now**, not “on-demand multi-symbol scan.”
- **Why didn’t this alert happen?**, not “forensic reconstruction endpoint.”
