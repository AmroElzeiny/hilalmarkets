# 💳 Business Model and Pricing

> **Workspace status:** Updated 17 July 2026. Product name: **HilalMarkets**.

## Revenue model

### Primary
B2C subscription SaaS for screened-market discovery, guided Watchlists, monitoring capacity, evidence history, diagnostics, and delivery.

### Later
- B2B screening and monitoring API.
- Approved broker, exchange, or Islamic-finance app integrations.
- Partner or white-label deployments after governance and product-market fit.
- Institutional evidence exports or compliance workflows where legally appropriate.

## Current public plan catalog

| Plan | Price | Intended user | Key current limits |
|---|---:|---|---|
| Free | $0/month | Learn the workflow | 1 saved/active Watchlist, 50 symbols per Watchlist, 10 alerts/day, 1 on-demand scan/month, Telegram |
| Core | $12/month | Active individual investor | 10 saved, 3 active, 200 symbols per Watchlist, 100 alerts/day, 20 investigations/month, 10 on-demand scans/month |
| Pro | $29/month | Advanced self-directed user | 50 saved, 10 active, broad symbol capacity, 500 alerts/day, deep forensics, Discord, advanced analytics and controls |

Only Free, Core, and Pro are publicly purchasable in the current catalog. Creator, Community, founder, legacy, and trial entries may exist internally but should not be exposed as purchasable through hidden browser values.

## Conditional trial concept

The current plan catalog includes a conditional 14-day monitoring cycle designed to renew until one qualifying alert is delivered, subject to product and legal approval. Trial behavior must be explained clearly and never imply guaranteed market opportunity.

## Packaging principles

- Sharia screening is foundational, not an expensive add-on.
- Paid tiers sell monitoring capacity, history, diagnostics, controls, and delivery—not religious certainty.
- Plan comparisons should use customer outcomes, not only large internal caps.
- Prices, currency, terms, and entitlements are server-owned.
- Verified provider events control paid access.
- Downgrades pause excess Watchlists without deleting data.
- Checkout must show recurring payment, renewal, cancellation, risk, and legal acknowledgements.

## Commercial hypotheses to validate

- Will users pay primarily for more Watchlists, wider coverage, or deeper evidence?
- Does Compliance Drift improve retention enough to be a core paid benefit?
- Do guided users convert to Pro for Advanced Controls?
- Is Arabic-language onboarding a growth lever or an expected baseline?
- Is Telegram sufficient for most individuals?
- Does methodology comparison increase willingness to pay, or mainly trust?
- Are B2B partners more interested in screening data, monitoring infrastructure, or both?

## Unit-economics worksheet

Track:

- average revenue per paying user;
- market-data and AI cost per active user;
- notification and infrastructure cost;
- reviewer/research cost per published asset;
- acquisition cost by channel;
- gross margin by plan;
- support cost per account;
- payback period;
- monthly and annual retention.

Do not report speculative unit economics externally as achieved performance.
