## Mission

## Vision

Become the trust and monitoring layer between Muslim investors and global financial markets.

## The problem

Muslim investors often combine several disconnected tools:

- a list or scholar source to ask whether an asset may be considered;
- project documents to understand why;
- a charting tool to inspect price behavior;
- alert tools to keep watching;
- messaging channels to receive updates;
- spreadsheets or notes to remember changes.

This fragmentation creates four pains:

1. **Eligibility uncertainty:** the status may be unexplained, outdated, methodology-specific, or disputed.
2. **Attention overload:** users cannot watch every screened asset and every chart continuously.
3. **Black-box alerts:** a trigger often lacks condition-level evidence or the status used at evaluation time.
4. **Compliance drift:** project activity, evidence, methodology, identity, or market availability can change after the initial review.

## The solution

HilalMarkets connects the workflow:

1. Select a disclosed Sharia methodology and policy.
2. Browse the Sharia-Screened Market.
3. Open an asset Evidence Passport.
4. Create a guided Watchlist or run Check the Market Now.
5. Evaluate only eligible assets using deterministic rules.
6. Follow the Opportunity Journey before and after an alert.
7. Inspect the exact market and Passport evidence.
8. Receive Compliance Drift updates when material facts change.

## What HilalMarkets is not

- Not a broker or exchange.
- Not an automatic trading or copy-trading platform.
- Not a provider of guaranteed returns.
- Not a universal religious authority.
- Not a generic Islamic lifestyle app.
- Not an unrestricted derivatives, leverage, lending, or yield platform.
- Not an AI oracle. AI can organize factual research and translate user intent, but cannot approve, publish, or execute.
