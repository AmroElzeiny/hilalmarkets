> **Workspace status:** Updated 17 July 2026. Product name: **HilalMarkets**.

## Brand core

**Category:** Sharia-screened market intelligence and monitoring  
**Audience:** self-directed Muslim crypto spot investors  
**Personality:** calm, rigorous, modern, respectful, transparent  
**Emotional outcome:** confidence without false certainty  
**Functional outcome:** less chart watching, clearer eligibility, explainable alerts

## Elevator pitches

### 10 words
Sharia-screened market intelligence and monitoring for self-directed Muslim investors.

### 30 seconds
HilalMarkets helps Muslim investors explore assets screened under transparent methodologies, choose the market changes they care about, and let the platform keep watch. It shows opportunities forming, explains every alert, and warns when an asset’s compliance status changes—without placing trades.

### Accelerator
HilalMarkets is building the trust and monitoring layer between Muslim investors and digital-asset markets. It connects versioned Sharia evidence to guided Watchlists, deterministic market evaluation, Opportunity Journeys, and Compliance Drift Alerts in one subscription platform.

## Voice principles

### Say
- “Screened as eligible under Methodology X, version Y.”
- “Reviewed on 12 July 2026.”
- “This use is not covered by the spot-asset assessment.”
- “Three of four requirements are present.”
- “The asset is paused while new evidence is reviewed.”
- “You make every investment decision.”

### Avoid
- “Definitely halal.”
- “AI-approved coin.”
- “Guaranteed alert.”
- “Best trade.”
- “Never miss an opportunity.”
- “Safe investment.”
- “Haram” when the stored fact is only “not covered,” “rejected for publication,” or “insufficient information.”
- “Ecosystem” repeated without explaining connected value.

## Visual identity

- Deep emerald: trust and eligibility.
- Warm ivory: calm background.
- Charcoal: serious financial clarity.
- Restrained gold: verified evidence or important guidance.
- Amber: qualification or under review.
- Red: exclusion or critical failure.
- Purple should not return as the dominant legacy visual language.
- Avoid generic mosque/minaret decoration; use evidence, paths, radar, layers, and status-change visuals.

## Messaging test

A first-time visitor should understand within five seconds:

1. it is for Muslim investors;
2. assets are screened under disclosed methodologies;
3. it monitors spot-market behavior;
4. the user does not need to code;
5. the platform does not trade automatically;
6. evidence exists behind status and alerts.
