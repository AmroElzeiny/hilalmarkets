          Keys and gestures

          Every one of these also has a button on screen. Nothing needs a mouse.

          Tab
          Move to the next control
          ↑ ↓
          Move between cards on the board
          ← →
          Go out to the group, or into its cards
          Enter
          Open the settings of the card you are on
          Tab
          Reach that card's own buttons, including “cancel the connection”
          Delete
          Remove that card. You can undo it
          Ctrl + Z
          Undo
          Ctrl + Shift + Z
          Redo
          Ctrl + Enter
          Open the list of conditions
          Ctrl + 0
          Fit the whole board on screen
          Esc
          Close what is open, leave full screen, or drop a line you are dragging without changing anything
          {#- What a pointer does. This was the one part of the board with no written explanation anywhere: dragging a wire off a card is how a connection is cancelled, and the only way to find that out was to try it. It is also what Hilal reads when somebody asks how to join or cancel a connection — it may only describe what the page itself says, so anything undocumented here is something it cannot help with. -#}

          With a mouse or a finger

          Drag the board
          Move around it. Nothing on the board changes
          Drag a card
          Move that card. Its connection stays where it was
          Drag the circle on a card's left edge onto a group
          Join the card to that group instead
          Drag that same circle onto empty space
          Cancel the connection. The card is set aside, keeping everything you typed
          Point at a line
          A small button appears on the line to cancel it
          Point at a set-aside card
          A button appears to join it back into the monitor
