How the screening works

A Shariah standard is a written set of rules. It says what a crypto project must do, and must not do, to pass. Each standard is published by the group behind it, and each one has a version number.

You choose the standard above the list. Choose one standard to see only the coins that passed it. Choose All to see every coin that passed at least one approved standard. A coin's Evidence Passport says which standard gave its result.

A result is a screening result under one standard and its version. It is not a fatwa, and it is not advice to buy or sell.

Why standards can disagree

Standards do not always agree. They can use different rules, or read the same evidence in a different way. So one coin can be Eligible under one standard and Excluded under another.

Hilal Markets keeps each standard's result separate, with its own evidence. A coin's Evidence Passport lets you switch between the standards that reviewed it.

What an Evidence Passport is

Every screened coin has one Evidence Passport. It is a public page with its own address, and you can open it without an account. It shows:

  • the coin's result under each standard that reviewed it;
  • the reasons the reviewer recorded;
  • the sources that were used, and the date each one was checked;
  • what the result covers, and which coin exactly was reviewed.

If something on a Passport looks wrong, you can report it from the Passport.
