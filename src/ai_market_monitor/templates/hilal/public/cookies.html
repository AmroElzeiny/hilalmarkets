Cookies and similar storage

Cookies and local storage can remember security state, preferences, and consent. Some are required to provide the requested service; optional categories require a choice.

Categories

Essential

Authentication, security, consent records, and core requested behavior. Essential storage remains on.

Analytics

Optional aggregated public-site use. It remains denied until consent and loads only when a Google Tag Manager container is explicitly configured.

Functional

Optional preferences beyond core operation. It remains denied until consent.

Marketing

Optional advertising measurement remains denied until explicit Marketing consent. When configured, the X Pixel or Meta Pixel may measure visits without receiving form values, account credentials, or private strategy content from Hilal Markets.

Consent choices

On first public visit, the banner offers Essential only, Customize, and Accept analytics. Marketing technologies remain off unless you enable Marketing in Customize. The choice is stored with version {{ cookie_consent_version }} and an ISO timestamp in first-party storage.

Google Consent Mode v2

Before any optional Google tag can load, ad storage, analytics storage, ad user data, ad personalization, functionality storage, and personalization storage default to denied; security storage defaults to granted. Consent is updated only after your choice.

Change or withdraw your choice

Open Cookie Settings below at any time. Withdrawal sends a denied consent update and replaces the stored choice. Already loaded scripts cannot be removed from browser memory, but no new optional provider is initialized on later page loads while consent remains denied.
