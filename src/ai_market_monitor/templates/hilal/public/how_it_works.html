The journey

Plain language first. Exact mechanics when needed.

The system helps translate your own idea. It does not invent a trade or choose "best" settings silently.

1

Explore the screened market

Begin with assets included by the selected methodology and allowed-status policy.

2

Inspect the Evidence Passport

Review status, methodology, date, qualifications, sources, and history.

3

Describe what to watch

Choose a guided starting point or explain measurable market behavior naturally.

4

Review the interpretation

Resolve ambiguities and inspect required rules, optional context, universe, timing, and policy.

5

Check the market once

See matches, forming candidates, non-matches, exclusions, and unavailable data without starting a monitor.

6

Approve continuous monitoring

Connect a notification channel, approve the immutable version, and follow every lifecycle update.

Your control

No strategy activates from AI prose.

Only validated registry capabilities and deterministic rule objects can enter a Watch Plan. Unresolved, unsupported, or provider-blocked requirements prevent approval.

Interpretation approved

Changes create a revision

Evidence throughout

Every meaningful result keeps its context.

While forming

See passed requirements, the current blocker, actual versus required values, and freshness.

When alerted

Receive immutable proof tied to the exact strategy and methodology versions.

When silent

Reconstruct market, policy, provider, cooldown, and delivery evidence.

Clear boundaries

Monitoring is not execution or advice.

HilalMarkets does not place trades, hold funds, request exchange trading keys, promise returns, or present a screening status as universal religious certainty.

You remain responsible for

Making your own financial and religious decisions
