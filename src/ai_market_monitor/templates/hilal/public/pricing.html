Billing questions

Know what you are paying for.

Plans increase capacity and product depth. They do not sell stronger religious certainty, guaranteed alerts, or expected returns.

Can I start without paying?

Yes. The Free plan and its enforced limits are shown above.

Can plan limits change?

The checkout and dashboard show the current catalog before purchase. Material changes should be communicated under the final terms.

Where do I manage billing?

Authenticated users manage their current plan and payment state in Plan & Billing.
