Scope

This draft explains how HilalMarkets handles information when you visit public pages, use product help, create an account, configure Watch Plans, receive alerts, contact support, or use related services.

Information that may be collected

  • Account and authentication details required to provide access.
  • Watch Plan definitions, approval records, market checks, lifecycle evidence, and delivery preferences.
  • Public product-help questions are audited as bounded hashes; inquiry details and contact information are stored only when you submit the inquiry form.
  • Support requests, safe diagnostics you approve, and incident records.
  • Consent choices and limited optional public-site analytics after consent.

How information may be used

  • Provide, secure, and improve the requested service.
  • Resolve screened universes and run approved Watch Plans.
  • Deliver opportunity and compliance notifications.
  • Respond to product inquiries and operate support.

Messaging providers

Telegram receives the recipient and message data needed for delivery only after you connect it. In-app records remain inside the authenticated dashboard.

Public product help

Your chat profile stays in the current browser session unless you choose device memory and Functional cookies are enabled. The browser transcript is not persisted by the public assistant. Submitted inquiries are emailed to you and the HilalMarkets office, protected by retention and redaction controls.

Analytics and private product data

Optional website analytics must not receive passwords, credentials, direct identifiers, raw private prompts, detailed strategy text, reviewer notes, support attachments, or asset-holding data. Consent choices are applied through the preference center before optional tags load.

Retention and deletion

Product-help answer audit events are retained for the configured bounded period without raw question text. Inquiry contact and content are retained for the configured support period and can be redacted through the token-bound control returned after submission. Other final legal retention periods and processor terms require verification before public launch.

Your choices and rights

You can change optional cookie choices from the footer. Applicable access, correction, deletion, portability, objection, and complaint rights depend on the verified legal entity and jurisdictions and must be finalized before launch.

Privacy contact

Use {{ privacy_email }} for privacy questions. The final policy must add verified controller identity and address before publication.
