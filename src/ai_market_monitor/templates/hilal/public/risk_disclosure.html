Purpose of HilalMarkets

HilalMarkets is a research, screening, and crypto spot market-monitoring service. It helps users explore methodology-screened assets, create Watchlists, observe opportunity states, receive alerts, and review evidence. It does not execute transactions or manage customer assets.

No religious, financial, legal, tax, or investment advice

Statuses, Watchlist results, alerts, charts, evidence summaries, and comparisons are information for research. They are not a fatwa, personal Sharia advice, financial recommendation, legal opinion, tax advice, or instruction to buy, sell, hold, stake, lend, borrow, or use an asset.

Digital-asset and market risk

Crypto assets can be highly volatile, illiquid, technically complex, and subject to rapid losses. Historical behavior, readiness, a forming opportunity, or an alert does not predict future performance.

Methodology-specific Sharia screening

A status means the asset met the effective policy under the displayed methodology, version, evidence set, and review date. It is not a universal declaration that every use of the asset is halal. Different qualified approaches may disagree, and a project can change after review.

Data, provider, and alert limitations

Market data can be late, incomplete, revised, unavailable, or inconsistent across providers. Networks, workers, schedules, and notification services can fail or delay an alert. Evidence and monitor-health views reduce uncertainty but cannot eliminate these risks.

Your responsibility

You remain responsible for reviewing the methodology, evidence, qualifications, Watchlist mechanics, provider state, and your own circumstances before making any decision.
