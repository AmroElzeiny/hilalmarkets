About HilalMarkets

Building the trust and monitoring layer for Muslim investors.

HilalMarkets connects transparent Sharia screening with explainable crypto spot monitoring so self-directed users can understand what they may consider, when their own conditions matter, and why.

Our mission

Make halal-conscious market participation easier to understand and monitor.

Muslim investors often move between a screening site, charting tools, alerts, and scattered project updates. HilalMarkets is designed to unite that journey without becoming a signal seller or automatic trading system.

What we believe

Trust comes from visible boundaries.

Explain the result

Show evidence, assumptions, versions, and missing data.

Respect governance

Technology assists; qualified people own religious review.

Keep users in control

No silent rule changes, approvals, or trade execution.

Show uncertainty

Unavailable, disputed, and under-review states stay visible.

Who we serve

The guided, active Muslim investor.

More active than a passive holder, but not trying to operate an institutional trading desk. The user values understandable defaults, evidence, and control.

What HilalMarkets is not

It is not a broker, custodian, exchange, execution bot, signal seller, fatwa service, or guarantee of market or religious outcomes.
