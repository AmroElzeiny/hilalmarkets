Your Gmail signature

Press the button. Then open Gmail and paste it in.

  1. Press Copy signature below.
  2. In Gmail, open Settings (the gear), then See all settings.
  3. Stay on the General tab and scroll down to Signature.
  4. Press Create new, give it a name, then click inside the big box.
  5. Paste with Ctrl + V (Cmd + V on a Mac).
  6. Scroll to the bottom of the page and press Save Changes.

The pictures are stored inside this file, so nothing needs to be online for this to work. Gmail uploads them for you when you paste. If the button does not work, select everything inside the dashed box by hand and copy it with Ctrl + C.
