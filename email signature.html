Then, in Gmail

  1. Open Settings (the gear), then See all settings.
  2. Stay on the General tab and scroll down to Signature.
  3. Press Create new, give it a name, then click inside the big box.
  4. Paste with Ctrl + V (Cmd + V on a Mac).
  5. Scroll to the bottom of the page and press Save Changes.

The logo is stored inside this file, so nothing needs to be online for this to work. Gmail uploads it for you when you paste. Outlook and Apple Mail take the same paste. If the button does not work, select everything inside the box above and copy it with Ctrl + C.
